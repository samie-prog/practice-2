About TechNova

TechNova is a platform for students to explore emerging technologies, learn new skills, and participate in creative technical activities. Students from different departments are welcome to take part in the event.

Activities and Highlights

  • Web Development and Coding Competition
  • Cybersecurity Awareness Workshop
  • Robotics and Innovation Exhibition
  • Quiz on Computer Science and Technology
  • Student Project Presentation
  • Prize Distribution and Closing Ceremony
